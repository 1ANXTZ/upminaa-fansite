Biography

Name Mina, better known as UpMinaa
From Germany 🇩🇪
Age 21 years old
Streaming since February 2023

UpMinaa's journey started when her Gawr Gura cosplay went viral and introduced her content to a much bigger audience. What started as a single cosplay moment became the beginning of a growing career combining cosplay, gaming and streaming.

Since launching her Twitch channel in February 2023, she has built a community through live streams, cosplay projects, gaming sessions and direct interaction with her viewers.

Fun Facts

  • She creates cosplay, gaming and entertainment content.
  • She communicates with her audience in German and English.
  • Anime-inspired characters are a major part of her cosplay style.
  • Gawr Gura and Hatsune Miku became some of her most recognized looks.
  • Her community follows both her streams and cosplay projects.
