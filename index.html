Biography

Name Mina, better known as UpMinaa
From Germany 🇩🇪
Age 20 years old
Streaming since February 2023

UpMinaa's journey began in late 2022, when a single Gawr Gura cosplay video went viral on TikTok. What was meant to be just a post turned into the start of a multi-platform career.

She officially launched her Twitch channel in February 2023 and built a community through streams, cosplay, gaming content and interaction with her audience.

Fun Facts

  • She's bilingual — switching between German and English on stream.
  • She blends cosplay with gaming and horror content.
  • Anime references are frequently present in her content.
  • Cosplays like Hatsune Miku and Gawr Gura became fan favorites.
  • Her streams usually happen multiple times per week.
