About Her

Upminaa is a German cosplayer and streamer known for her detailed character portrayals, creative costumes and engaging personality. She creates cosplay content inspired by anime, games and pop culture, sharing her work with a growing online community. Besides cosplay, she is also active through live streams, where she interacts with fans, plays games and builds a welcoming community around her content.
