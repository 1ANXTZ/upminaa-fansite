About

Who is Upminaa?

Name

Mina

Known As

Upminaa

Content

Cosplay, streaming and gaming

Origin

Germany

Upminaa is a German cosplayer and content creator known for combining anime-inspired cosplay, gaming content and livestream interactions with her community.

Her journey grew through platforms such as TikTok, Twitch and other social networks, where she shares character transformations, cosplay photography and moments with fans around the world.

This fan hub was created as a portfolio project to organize information, showcase her work and present her online presence in a clean and respectful way.
